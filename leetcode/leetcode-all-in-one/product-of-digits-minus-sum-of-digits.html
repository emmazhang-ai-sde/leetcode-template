Problem

Given a positive integer n, calculate the difference between the product of its digits and the sum of its digits.

Note: order matters — the answer is product - sum (not sum - product).

Example

  • For n = 123456, the output is solution(n) = 699.
    • Product of digits: 1 * 2 * 3 * 4 * 5 * 6 = 720.
    • Sum of digits: 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6 = 21.
    • Final answer: 720 - 21 = 699.
  • For n = 1010, the output is solution(n) = -2.
    • Product of digits: 1 * 0 * 1 * 0 = 0.
    • Sum of digits: 1 + 0 + 1 + 0 = 2.
    • Final answer: 0 - 2 = -2.
